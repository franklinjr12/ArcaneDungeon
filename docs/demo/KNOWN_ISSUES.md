# Known issues

You do not need to report these. We already know about them.

- **Frame rate may dip.** We have not yet confirmed smooth 60 FPS on a typical gaming PC. If it dips, tell us your CPU, graphics card, screen resolution, and the Lighting setting (Settings > Video). Say what was happening on screen. Lighting Low may help.
- **Art and audio are placeholders.** Most sprites, tiles and effects are temporary. Every sound is still a temporary or silent placeholder.
- **Balance is unfinished.** Mana now runs low fast, so one spell spammed over and over runs dry. Mixing elements gives mana back. Fights against the new enemies and bosses can be hard, and our test bots almost never finish a run. Some spells and enemies may still be too strong or too weak. Run length varies a lot.
- **Compendium page for enemies may look odd.** The footer counter can read "0 / 17" even when you have met enemies.
- **The Windows build is unsigned.** Windows SmartScreen may warn you when you start it. Choose "More info", then "Run anyway", if you trust where you got the file.
- **Keyboard and mouse only.** There is no gamepad support.
- **English only.**

## Report anything else

Press F8 in the game, type one line and save. Then fill in the feedback form and send both back the same way the demo was shared with you.
